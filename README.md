🚀 DevStack Builder
Build Your Perfect Technology Stack

<p> A modern and interactive <strong>Technology Stack Builder</strong> built with <strong>React</strong> and <strong>TypeScript</strong>. </p>

<p> Explore technologies, compare their details, and build your own personalized development stack with just a few clicks. </p>

</div>

✨ Features

<table> <tr> <td width="50%"> <h3>🔍 Explore Technologies</h3> <p> Browse different technologies with their icons, descriptions, categories, difficulty levels, and ratings. </p> </td> <td width="50%"> <h3>🧩 Build Your Own Stack</h3> <p> Add technologies to your personal stack and see your selected technologies instantly. </p> </td> </tr>

<tr> <td width="50%"> <h3>🗑️ Manage Your Stack</h3> <p> Remove individual technologies or clear the entire stack with a single click. </p> </td> <td width="50%"> <h3>🔔 Interactive Notifications</h3> <p> Get instant notifications when adding, removing, or clearing technologies from your stack. </p> </td> </tr>

<tr> <td colspan="2"> <h3>⚡ Dynamic Data Loading</h3> <p> Technology information is loaded asynchronously from a JSON data source using React Suspense. </p> </td> </tr> </table>
